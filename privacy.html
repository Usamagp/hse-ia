HSE Improvement Action app - Privacy policy

This is an internal tool used by the HSE team to report unsafe acts and conditions at our facility.

What the app uses

  • Your Google account email address - to check that you are an authorised team member.
  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send) - only to send the improvement action you create to the HSE inbox. The app cannot read, delete or change any of your emails.
  • Photos and form details you enter - saved with the improvement action in the HSE department's Google Drive and Google Sheet.

What the app does not do

  • It does not read your mailbox, contacts or files.
  • It does not share your data with anyone outside the HSE reporting process, and does not sell or use it for advertising.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. HSE can also remove you from the members list.
